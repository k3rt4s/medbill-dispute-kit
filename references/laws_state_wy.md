## Unfair claims settlement practices

[W.S. § 26-13-124](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title26.pdf), verified 2026-09-07, regulates unfair claims practices performed with frequency indicating a general business practice. A standalone private remedy under that section remains unverified; use the regulator route rather than asserting one.

## Bad-faith remedy

[W.S. § 26-15-124(a), (c)](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title26.pdf), verified 2026-09-07, generally requires acceptance/payment or rejection within 45 days after proof of loss and supporting evidence, subject to its exceptions. A court rendering judgment for a claimant may award attorney fees and 10% annual interest for unreasonable or causeless refusal to pay a covered loss. Common-law elements remain a gap. Employer-plan preemption requires legal review under [29 U.S.C. § 1144](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/1144), verified 2026-09-07.

## State surprise-billing statute

A broader Wyoming statute was not verified. Federal NSA coverage and exceptions are explained in the [CMS insured-patient guide](https://www.cms.gov/initiatives/your-patient-rights/medical-bill-rights/know-your-medical-bill-rights/know-your-rights-insurance), verified 2026-09-07. Confirm service and plan coverage before asserting a protected balance bill.

## Ground-ambulance protection

Wyoming-specific protection remains unresolved. CMS says ground ambulance generally falls outside federal NSA billing protection, subject to state law. Source: [CMS guide](https://www.cms.gov/initiatives/your-patient-rights/medical-bill-rights/know-your-medical-bill-rights/know-your-rights-insurance), verified 2026-09-07. Do not convert search silence into a statement that Wyoming has no protection.

## State attorney general consumer protection contact

[Wyoming Attorney General, Consumer Protection and Antitrust Unit](https://ag.wyo.gov/law-office-division/consumer-protection-and-antitrust-unit), verified 2026-09-07, publishes the complaint route and <ag.consumer@wyo.gov>. The unit enforces the state Consumer Protection Act and cannot act as the patient's private lawyer. Confirm current postal/telephone details and whether the unit accepts the particular dispute before filing.

## Small claims court

The [Judicial Branch small-claims guide](https://www.wyocourts.gov/legal-help-by-topic/small-claims/), as published on 2026-09-07, states a $6,000 ceiling and a $10 filing fee, with service costs additional. A lawyer is permitted but not required; there is no jury at the small-claims hearing. The [court-system overview](https://www.wyocourts.gov/about-the-courts/), verified 2026-09-07, places small claims in circuit court. Confirm county scheduling, service and filing instructions before use.

## Statute of limitations

[W.S. § 1-3-105(a)(i)-(ii)](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title01.pdf), verified 2026-09-07, gives ten years for written contracts and eight for nonwritten express or implied contracts. This matches the kit's WY 10/8 row. Accrual, exceptions and effects of payment/acknowledgment require case-specific review.

## Medical-debt credit-reporting protections

A Wyoming-specific medical-debt reporting prohibition remains unverified. Federal [15 U.S.C. § 1681i](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681i), verified 2026-09-07, provides a report-accuracy dispute mechanism, not a blanket reporting prohibition.

## Charity care and state-level FAP requirements

Additional state requirements were not verified. Covered charitable hospitals have policy, charge and collection requirements under [26 U.S.C. § 501(r)(4)-(6)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/501), verified 2026-09-07. Request the hospital's actual policy and criteria; do not invent an income threshold or assert that no state requirement exists.

## Hospital lien statute

[W.S. § 26-15-136](https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title26.pdf), verified 2026-09-07, addresses assignment of insurance proceeds and a state lien for care, with notice provisions. It does not establish a general private-hospital tort-settlement lien in this pack. The existence and scope of such a lien remain unresolved; obtain the claimed statutory basis before responding.

## Key Wyoming-specific advantages

1. Section 26-15-124 supplies a claims-processing deadline with stated exceptions to check against an insurer's chronology.
2. Its conditional fee-and-interest remedy can support review of an unreasonable refusal, without inventing a common-law damages theory.
3. Circuit-court small claims permits self-representation and a published low entry fee where the amount and procedure fit.

These are practical uses of the dated sources above, not guarantees of recovery.
