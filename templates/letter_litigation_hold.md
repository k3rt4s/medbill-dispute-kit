# Template, Litigation hold notice

Use when the patient reasonably anticipates litigation over a disputed medical-billing matter and needs the provider or collector to preserve the evidence before filing. Common triggers:

- The patient has sent a formal 30-day warning and is preparing to file.
- The provider or collector has not substantively resolved a documented dispute.
- A filing date, court deadline, or attorney consultation makes the dispute likely to enter litigation.
- The patient has reason to believe relevant records may be revised, deleted, or overwritten in ordinary operations.

This notice adds a preservation step after the 30-day warning and before filing. It requests records preservation and written confirmation; it does not make threats or state an unverified state-law sanction.

```letter
[PATIENT FULL NAME]
[STREET ADDRESS]
[CITY, STATE ZIP]
Phone: [PATIENT PHONE]
Email: [PATIENT EMAIL]

[DATE]

[BILLING / LEGAL DEPARTMENT]
[PROVIDER NAME]

USPS Tracking: [CERTIFIED MAIL TRACKING NUMBER]

RE: Preservation notice for reasonably anticipated litigation
    Account #[ACCOUNT NUMBER]
    Patient: [PATIENT FULL NAME]
    Date(s) of service: [DATE OF SERVICE]
    Balance asserted: $[CURRENT BALANCE]
    Litigation reasonably anticipated as of: [DATE LITIGATION BECAME REASONABLY ANTICIPATED]

To Billing / Legal Department:

Litigation concerning the above account is reasonably anticipated as of [DATE LITIGATION BECAME REASONABLY ANTICIPATED]. Please preserve all potentially relevant records and electronically stored information in your possession, custody, or control. This request concerns the account and dates of service stated above and any related dispute, billing, collection, insurance, or financial-assistance activity under [STATE] law.

Please suspend ordinary deletion, overwriting, retention, and disposal processes for relevant materials, including materials held in electronic health record, billing, coding, document-management, patient-portal, call-recording, email, text-message, collection, and backup systems.

Please preserve, without alteration:

1. The itemized bill and every version or revision of it.
2. Each UB-04 or CMS-1500 claim form as submitted.
3. All Explanation of Benefits documents, remittance advice, and claim-adjudication records.
4. The complete medical record for the dates of service.
5. Coding records, charge-capture material, and audit notes.
6. The chargemaster that was in effect on each date of service.
7. Each version of the financial-assistance policy and my financial-assistance application file.
8. All collection-account notes and call recordings.
9. Every communication with me, including letters, portal messages, emails, text messages, and internal communications concerning this account.

Please provide written confirmation within 14 calendar days that the identified materials and systems have been preserved and that relevant auto-deletion has been suspended. This is a requested confirmation window, not a statement of a statutory deadline.

Please direct written confirmation to the address above. I reserve all rights and will continue to communicate in writing.

Sincerely,



[PATIENT FULL NAME]

Account number: [ACCOUNT NUMBER]
Date(s) of service: [DATE OF SERVICE]

cc: [COLLECTION AGENCY NAME AND MAILING ADDRESS, IF INVOLVED]
```

---

## Placeholders and rendering notes

- Use `[PATIENT FULL NAME]`, `[STREET ADDRESS]`, `[CITY, STATE ZIP]`, `[PATIENT PHONE]`, `[PATIENT EMAIL]`, `[DATE]`, `[BILLING / LEGAL DEPARTMENT]`, `[PROVIDER NAME]`, `[PROVIDER MAILING ADDRESS]`, `[CERTIFIED MAIL TRACKING NUMBER]`, `[ACCOUNT NUMBER]`, `[DATE OF SERVICE]`, and `[CURRENT BALANCE]` only from the patient's records and current correspondence. Leave any unresolved value as its placeholder.
- `[DATE LITIGATION BECAME REASONABLY ANTICIPATED]` must be tied to a real event, such as the date the patient decided to file after an unresolved 30-day warning. Do not invent it.
- `[STATE]` identifies the jurisdiction only. Do not add a state preservation citation or claim a state sanction unless it has been verified for that state with a URL and verification date.
- `[COLLECTION AGENCY NAME AND MAILING ADDRESS, IF INVOLVED]` is included only when a collector is involved; otherwise omit the `cc:` line rather than supplying a fictional recipient.
- `[CERTIFIED MAIL TRACKING NUMBER]` remains unresolved until the patient mails the letter.
- Source checked for federal scope only: Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 37(e), including its 2015 committee note, <https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp/rule_37>, verified 2026-09-07. It addresses electronically stored information that should have been preserved in anticipation or conduct of federal litigation; it is not a default state-law citation or a statement of a state sanction.

## Before sending

1. Confirm that litigation is reasonably anticipated and record the actual triggering event and date.
2. Confirm the provider and, if applicable, collector mailing addresses from current correspondence.
3. Attach no medical records unless needed; preserve the patient's copies and send only the notice by certified mail.
4. Check the relevant state preservation authority before adding any state-specific citation or remedy language.

## Parallel actions (same day as mailing)

- Save a complete copy of the signed notice and the certified-mail receipt with the dispute file.
- Send the same notice to the collector when one is handling the account.
- Preserve the patient's bills, itemizations, EOBs, correspondence, and call log without editing the originals.
- Record the mailing date and tracking number in the patient's action log or case notes.

## Follow-up

- After 14 calendar days without confirmation, keep the receipt and nonresponse in the case file; do not infer that a preservation duty was rejected or waived.
- If the dispute remains unresolved after the 30-day warning, prepare the appropriate court filing using `templates/small_claims_civil_warrant.md` and include this notice in the evidence file.
- If a provider or collector produces records, compare them with the patient's retained versions and document any material discrepancy.
